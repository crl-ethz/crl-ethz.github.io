The [Computational Robotics Lab (CRL)](https://crl.ethz.ch) is a research group within the [Department of Computer Science](https://inf.ethz.ch/) at [ETH Zurich](https://www.ethz.ch/en.html). CRL is led by [Prof. Dr. Stelian Coros](https://crl.ethz.ch/people/coros/index.html).

We are looking for bright, motivated and creative PhD students and Postdocs who are eager to get involved in cutting edge research in our group. If you are interested, please send an email to [crl-jobs@inf.ethz.ch](mailto:crl-jobs@inf.ethz.ch) with your CV and a description of your research interests. Please note that due to the large volume of requests, we can only respond to applicants who are considered for interviews.
